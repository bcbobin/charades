Delete your data

Holiday Charades, by Bogdan Bobin

Holiday Charades has no accounts. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you send with Suggest a word: the word, and a name and email address if you filled them in.

Delete your suggestions, name or email

  1. Email contact@bogdanbobin.com with the subject "Holiday Charades: delete my data".
  2. Include the email address or name you used, and the words you suggested if you remember them.
  3. We delete every matching suggestion, name and email address within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If your word was already added to the game, it stays in the game, but your name is removed from its card.

Delete what's on your device

Scores, team names, settings and a saved name or email are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes them.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
