Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Holiday Charades app for Android and iOS and the website charadesnight.com. Holiday Charades is a party game played on one device. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Scores, team names, the timer length and which cards you have already seen are saved on your device so the game can pick up where you left off. If you fill in a name or email address in "Suggest a word", they are also saved on your device so you don't have to type them again. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes it.

What you can choose to send

If you use "Suggest a word", the game sends what you type in that form: the word or phrase, its holiday, category and difficulty, and, only if you fill them in, a name to credit and an email address. It is sent over an encrypted connection and stored in a private Google Sheet that only the developer can open.

  • The word is used to decide whether to add it to the game.
  • The name is shown on the card, as credit, if your word is added.
  • The email address is used only to tell you when your word is added.

We don't sell, share or rent this information, and we don't use it for advertising. "Suggest a word" is optional; the game works fully without it.

Deleting your data

To have a suggestion, name or email address removed, follow the steps on Delete your data or email contact@bogdanbobin.com.

The website

charadesnight.com loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page shows a Ko-fi button. Like any website, those services receive your IP address when your browser loads them. The app doesn't use either: its fonts are built in.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us their name or email address, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
